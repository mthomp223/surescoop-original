About Surescoop

The Surescoop was created to fill the need that exists to be able to accurately and conveniently measure equine feed. The Surescoop is designed to measure both sweet and pelletted feeds. The feed manufacturer's recommendations can be followed with confidence using the 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and 1 pound increments. The Surescoop allows the horse owner/manager to feed the horse(s) s/he cares for properly so that the nutritional requirements of the horse(s) are met. The Surescoop is made in the USA of a durable plastic that will handle the daily demands of the barn with no problem.
